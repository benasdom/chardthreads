How a garment is made here

Four passes. One fit.

  1. 01

    Measure

    Thirty-one points on your actual body, not a size chart. We write it down once and keep it for life.

  2. 02

    Cut

    A paper pattern drafted to your measure, chalked onto cloth, then cut by hand along the line.

  3. 03

    Stitch

    Seams closed by hand where hand-work holds better; machine where machine-work holds better.

  4. 04

    Fit

    A second fitting before the final press. If it doesn't sit right, it isn't finished yet.
